# Preview homepage surface brief

Mode: Persuade. The page serves international STEM students and new graduates evaluating whether Zhiyin can organize a complete job-search workflow. The primary action is to open the existing Career Profile; the supporting action moves to product evidence. Existing product routes, terminology, purple brand recognition, and all business behavior stay intact. Demonstrations are labeled as demonstration data.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Treat the homepage as an evidence dossier that progressively proves one continuous career workflow. Avoid the category-standard wall of equal feature cards and unsupported metric bands.

OWN-WORLD: Crisp warm-white sheets, deep ink, brand violet used as a committed field, cyan reserved for confirmed safe or active states, square-to-soft geometry, registration marks, hairline rules, and dense but readable product annotations.

STORY: The visitor recognizes the fragmented job-search problem, sees how one Career Profile carries context across the workflow, verifies the live product routes, understands control and privacy boundaries, then starts a profile.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact custom navigation sits above a two-column hero. The left holds one direct promise, concise proof language, and two clear actions. The right is a large annotated product canvas showing Today, job matching, resume, and interview context as one connected workspace.

FORM: Editorial evidence sheet, fifth structure in the ordered set, selected from the roll led by seed 47e1ca61. Its signature interaction is a proof rail whose links open the real product surfaces rather than simulating unavailable results.
